Tilemaker’s Workshop

A Carcassonne-style sandbox. Lay one tile a turn; edges must agree — road meets road, city meets city, field meets field.

  • Place — drag the tile from the panel onto a glowing square, or click one. R rotates.
  • Claim — after placing, put a meeple on a road, city or field to score it.
  • Score — finished roads and cities pay out; the biggest map wins nothing, the smartest one does.

Drag to pan · scroll to zoom · everything else is in the panel on the right.
